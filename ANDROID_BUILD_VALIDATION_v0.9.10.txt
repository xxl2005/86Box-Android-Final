86Box Android v0.9.10 - Touchpad Gesture Validation
===================================================

Scope
-----
Touch-only update on top of v0.9.9. Physical mouse auto-capture and VM picker keepalive are unchanged.

Implemented
-----------
- One-finger swipe: relative guest mouse movement (existing path retained).
- Single short tap: one left click.
- Double short tap: two distinct left-click gestures for guest double-click handling.
- One-finger stationary hold >= 2000 ms: right click while held.
- Release after long press does not emit a left click.
- Existing two-finger tap right click retained.
- Existing pinch zoom / two-finger pan retained.
- Touch setting text updated to describe all gestures.

------------
1. Structural/source sanity: PASS.
2. Gesture regression harness + SDL event integration + clean diff: PASS.
3. Package/version/ZIP integrity: performed when COMPLETE ZIP is generated.
